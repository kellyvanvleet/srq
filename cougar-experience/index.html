04 / Technology with a human purpose

More personal. Built to last.

Use technology and automation to support a personalized experience across the whole journey, without asking staff to create every interaction from scratch.

The goal is to systematically use technology to increase meaningful engagement at every stage, while keeping staff focused on the personal connections that matter.

Understand the family.

Use interests, preferences, and needs families choose to share to make communication relevant.

Support the moment.

Connect timely information, helpful reminders, and personal check-ins to where each family is in its journey.

Make it sustainable.

Review existing tools first. Use automation and AI thoughtfully, with staff judgment and personal relationships at the center.

05 / Insight into improvement

Measure what matters across the family journey.

Four connected performance areas show whether we're reaching families, helping them enroll, keeping them involved, and building lasting relationships.

01 / RECRUITMENT

Are we reaching families?

Core measure: Qualified inquiries by source.

Also track event interest, outreach response, and which channels bring families into the pipeline.

02 / ENROLLMENT

Are interested families enrolling?

Core measure: Inquiry-to-enrollment conversion.

Track each step: inquiry → visit → application → acceptance → enrollment, and where families disengage.

03 / ENGAGEMENT

Are students and families participating?

Core measure: Student and family participation over time.

Track involvement in school life, program participation, meaningful communication responses, and feedback on belonging.

04 / RETENTION & RELATIONSHIPS

Are families staying connected?

Core measure: Year-to-year student retention.

Understand re-enrollment, reasons families leave, satisfaction, and alumni involvement after graduation.

Start with existing school data. Set a baseline, identify where the experience breaks down, test improvements, and measure whether they increase engagement and long-term connection.
