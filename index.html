01My story

I care about the whole experience.

I started on the physical side of business, working across manufacturing, logistics, purchasing, supply chain, packaging and innovation. That taught me how products actually get made, how businesses make money, and how decisions in one part of a company affect everything downstream.

I took that foundation into healthcare technology and digital health, where I helped build products, teams and the operating structure around them. I’ve worked from early ideas and MVPs through implementation, launch, customer experience and scale.

I can walk the walk and talk the talk because I’ve built it, launched it, operated it, fixed it and helped grow the people and teams around it. I care deeply about the work, I have strong taste, I’m usually the culture person, and I believe serious work can still be fun.

03How I work

Understand the problem. Find the right solution.

I don’t believe AI or automation is the answer to everything. I believe in understanding the problem first, then finding the best solution.

Sometimes that’s technology. Sometimes it’s process, people, product or simply doing something differently.

Human first.
I believe businesses are built by people. I build teams, develop people and create cultures where people care about the work and each other. AI can make great people more capable, but it doesn’t replace judgment, creativity, relationships or leadership.
