03How I work

Understand first.
Then decide what needs fixing.

I don’t start with the solution. I get inside the business, understand the people, product, customer and workflow, then figure out what will actually move it forward.

Learn

Get close to the people, product, customer and context.

Map

See the workflow, handoffs, friction and dependencies.

Prioritize

Separate what matters from what is merely noisy.

Build

Create the process, product, tool or operating model.

Enable

Give people what they need to use it well.

Support

Stay close enough to see what breaks in reality.

Measure

Look for impact, not activity for activity’s sake.

Improve

Keep learning and make the next version better.
