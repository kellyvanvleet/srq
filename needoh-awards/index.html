01 / The original idea

Not another
boring awards ceremony.

Everyone gets a QR code in their hotel gift bag. It opens a guessing game where they match funny NeeDoh-inspired appreciation awards to employees. During the meeting presentation, the recipient and matching NeeDoh are revealed. The toy is handed to the person on stage.

Why these toys

They started as a summer of early-morning NeeDoh hunting with James. The collection became a labor of love, and the idea was to turn that into a memorable thank-you.

“When work makes you want to squeeze something, squeeze this and think of me.”
